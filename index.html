A paragraph is a group of related sentences that develop a single idea, typically beginning with a topic sentence and followed by supporting details and a concluding sentence. Paragraphs help organize writing by breaking it into logical, digestible parts, making it easier for the reader to follow the progression of ideas and grasp the main points. The structure of a paragraph can vary, but it should always remain coherent and focused on one central theme.

Topic Sentence: This is the first sentence of the paragraph and introduces the main idea or controlling idea.

Supporting Sentences: These sentences provide details, evidence, examples, and explanations to support the topic sentence.

Concluding Sentence: This final sentence summarizes the main point of the paragraph or provides a transition to the next topic.
